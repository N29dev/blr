Fair Play & Responsible Use

Cheating, exploiting and unauthorized modification of Roblox are prohibited and may lead to account suspension or permanent bans. Replay Studio is intended solely for authorized post-match analysis, coaching, content creation and educational purposes. It does not interact with live Roblox gameplay.

Only import recordings you are authorized to collect and share. This independent community tool is not affiliated with Roblox or the Blue Lock: Rivals development team.

Roblox Community Standards · Roblox anti-cheat policy · BL:R Tech Rules

The included demo is synthetic, not a real match; its goal timestamp is an illustrative scoreboard update. Imported ZIP and JSON files are processed in your browser, without upload. Optional avatar appearance sends only public Roblox UserIds (or legacy usernames) to the Glutony Worker and loads public thumbnails or public outfit assets from Roblox. It is disabled for live streams. Current avatar appearance may differ from the recorded match. The site-wide visitor counter uses anonymous browser presence, respects browser privacy preferences, and is disabled for personal live links. Account connections remain disabled. Personal live viewing is available only through a personal stream link. Recorded usernames stay in the imported files and browser memory; only manually edited labels may be saved in local browser storage. No game capture or upload is provided. Use supported Studio APIs or recordings explicitly authorized by the game developers.
