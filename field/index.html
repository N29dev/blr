Field boundaries

An invisible barrier follows the white outer perimeter of the pitch. Both players and the ball are prevented from leaving the field. The dashed amber overlay marks the reported collision boundary, not a visible in-game line. This diagram does not simulate goal handling, bounce behavior or barrier height.

Source: direct gameplay observation supplied by N29, November 24, 2026.

Measurement basis

YK estimated the pitch at 540 × 360 in-game meters using Zantetsu’s Jet Counter as an approximately 60 m ruler. The report estimates ±5% uncertainty. Its stated arc correction of 0.6 m evaluates to approximately 0.054 m using its own formula.

The pitch boundary in the supplied overhead screenshot measures approximately 1,468 × 1,100 pixels (1.335:1), while the research estimate is 3:2. The diagram preserves the screenshot proportions, including the 24-sided center circle, stripe alignment and box locations. The measurement tool provisionally maps the two axes independently to 540 × 360 game meters; a circular range therefore appears slightly elliptical. If the screenshot is orthographic and the 540 m length is correct, its proportions imply a width near 405 m instead. Further in-game measurements are needed to resolve this discrepancy. Game meters are not necessarily real-world meters.
