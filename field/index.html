Field Lab

Measure distances and visualize ability ranges on the BL:R pitch.

Tap two points on the pitch to measure their straight-line distance.

Pitch size540 × 360 m

Keyboard: focus the pitch and use arrow keys to move the cursor; Enter selects a point. Measurements use the research estimate, not verified game coordinates.

Measurement basis

YK estimated the pitch at 540 × 360 in-game meters using Zantetsu’s Jet Counter as an approximately 60 m ruler. The report estimates ±5% uncertainty. Its stated arc correction of 0.6 m evaluates to approximately 0.054 m using its own formula.

Field markings are reconstructed approximately from the supplied overhead screenshot, with the center Ubers obstruction omitted. The screenshot’s apparent aspect ratio differs from the research estimate, so the diagram uses the reported 3:2 dimensions; markings are not independently measured. Game meters are not necessarily real-world meters.
