Privacy Policy

Effective: November 4, 2026. N29 BLR Companion is an unofficial community website, not affiliated with Roblox or Blue Lock: Rivals.

What information is processed

Public server browsing does not require a user account. If you optionally sign in with Roblox, Roblox provides your user ID, username, display name, and optional avatar image through its OAuth service. The website uses this information to display your connected profile.

Authentication and cookies

We use an HttpOnly, Secure session cookie to keep you signed in. A temporary cookie helps complete the Roblox authorization process. The initial session expires after approximately 12 hours. Roblox access and refresh tokens are not intentionally retained after the login process.

Data storage

The Roblox login implementation stores a signed profile session in your browser cookie; it does not create a persistent Roblox profile record in our database. If you choose to report a server region, we store the reported broad region, the public server ID and place ID, a timestamp, and a pseudonymous HMAC identifier derived from your Roblox user ID. We use that identifier to limit duplicate and excessive reports without storing your raw Roblox ID with the reports. Community reports are used for approximately six hours and may be retained for operational cleanup. Basic operational and security logs may be processed by hosting providers. If saved preferences or friends features are added, this policy will be updated before collecting additional information.

Third-party services

Authentication is provided by Roblox; website and API infrastructure is provided by Cloudflare and GitHub Pages. Public server and optional region information may involve Roblox and external server-metadata providers. Their privacy policies also apply to their respective services.

Your choices

You may browse without signing in or use Sign out to clear the website session. You can revoke the application's access through your Roblox account settings. For privacy inquiries or removal requests relating to future stored account information, use the support contact linked on the website.

Changes

We may update this policy when functionality changes. The updated effective date will be shown on this page.
