THE MAIN RULE

Unfair advantage = punishable.

Tech or bugs that give the triggering player an unfair advantage may be banned. Examples include unintended speed boosts, teleports, flying, increased shot power or range, automatic goals, and advantage-granting emote bugs.

QUESTIONS & REPORTS

Before you use a tech.

Rules can change. The official support team decides individual cases.

Can I appeal a bug-abuse ban?

Yes. Players may appeal a bug-abuse ban whether they knowingly triggered the bug or not. First join the BL:R support server ↗, then open the appeals channel ↗ to explain your situation. Appeals are reviewed by staff and are not guaranteed to succeed.

How do I know if a technique is allowed?

Join the BL:R support server ↗ and open a tech inquiry ticket ↗. Provide a clip, how to trigger the technique, and its gameplay impact so support staff can review it.

Are the allowed and disallowed lists final?

No. Allowed tech can become disallowed if it becomes an issue. Punishable bugs remain punishable. Check official announcements for changes.

What if my tech or bug isn't listed?

For tech inquiries, consult the original moderation FAQ ↗. For bug inquiries, contact its author or support staff with a clip, reproduction steps, and relevant details. An unlisted exploit is not automatically approved.

What bugs are generally bannable?

Any bug granting an unfair gameplay advantage may be punishable, including unintended speed, teleports, flight, extended shots, automatic goals, and gameplay-altering emote glitches. The lists above are examples, not exhaustive exemptions.
